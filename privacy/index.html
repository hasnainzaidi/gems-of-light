Privacy policy

Learning stays private.

Gems of Light is designed so a child can play without creating an account or giving us personal information.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Who operates Gems of Light

Gems of Light is published and operated by Ashna Holdings, LLC. This policy applies to the Gems of Light website and progressive web app at playgemsoflight.com.

Information we do not collect through the app

Gems of Light does not require accounts and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or in-app forms. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Information stored on your device

Learning progress, settings, and practice history are stored locally in the browser on the device being used. We do not receive that local progress data. The app also uses a service worker to cache game and audio files on the device so it can load reliably and support offline play.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the site data for playgemsoflight.com in the browser, or by removing the installed web app and its site data.

Network and service-provider information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may process routine technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as an IP address, browser type, requested pages, and request times.

The app may request fonts from Google Fonts and, when a local recitation file is unavailable, audio from EveryAyah. Those services may receive routine request information under their own privacy practices. We do not use those requests to build advertising profiles.

Children’s privacy

Gems of Light is intended for use with a parent or grown-up. We do not knowingly ask children to submit names, email addresses, precise locations, photos, voice recordings, or other personal information through the web app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email or another channel, contact us and we will review and delete it where appropriate.

Contact us

A parent or guardian can send privacy questions or requests to developer@playgemsoflight.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s information practices change, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date before the new practices take effect where required.
