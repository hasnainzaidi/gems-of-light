Privacy

Gems of Light · effective 15 August 2026 · applies to playgemsoflight.com and the Gems of Light iOS app

Gems of Light collects no data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything your child does in the game stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The game remembers your child's journey — which surahs they have gathered, their settings, how their gardens have grown — in your browser's (or the app's) local storage. This save never leaves the device. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else. Deleting the game's website data (or the app) deletes the save.

What the game never asks for

  • No account, sign-in, name, or email
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No third-party SDKs, and no in-app purchases

Children

Gems of Light is made for children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There is nothing to consent to, review, or delete — the data simply never exists.

The web version's few network requests

Playing at playgemsoflight.com, your browser fetches the game's own files from our host, its two typefaces from Google Fonts, and — when a recitation is not yet saved for offline play — audio from everyayah.com, the recitation archive. These are ordinary web requests: the servers involved see standard connection details (such as an IP address) to deliver the files, and we attach nothing to them — no cookies, no identifiers. Once heard, recitations are kept on the device and play offline.

The iOS app carries everything inside it — game, typefaces, and every recitation — so it plays fully offline and needs no network at all.

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that touches privacy, this page will say so plainly before it does, with a new effective date. Our promise is the first line above, and it is not planned to change.
