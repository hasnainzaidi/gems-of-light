Five highest-impact fixes

Implementation status: all five fix groups below are complete in the working tree; the original priorities are retained for traceability.

1 · Recompose Al-Qadr's waterfallsW04-F01 · world-specific · first

Give all three shafts sources and intentional receivers; two currently stop on dry ground.

2 · Recompose Al-Lail's waterfallsW07-F01 · world-specific · second

Make the long climb's three falls belong to the sole valley pool or clearly magical mist systems.

3 · Ground skyline landmarksW13-F01, W17-F01, W20-F01 · repeated authoring pattern

Seat landmarks into the far horizon while preserving their small, non-collidable character.

4 · Recover the One LightW09-F01 · world-specific

Make the central pillar identifiable before and after arrival without turning it into a solid wall.

5 · Separate reflection from routeW18-F01 · world-specific

Use stronger water and inversion cues so the reflected road cannot resemble a second bridge.

Retest all five at the modern native viewport (874×402 on the available iPhone 17 Pro) and the nearest SE landscape viewport. Water fixes should be judged from both the valley/approach and the upper/far side.

  • W01 Al-Falaq: gentle elevation rhythm, contained pond, clear garden-to-rest progression.
  • W02 An-Nas: recognizable dooryard, rooftops, fountain crossing, lane, and village edge.
  • W03 Al-'Adiyat: long route broken into dunes, water crossings, caravan road, ridge, and quiet arrival.
  • W05 Al-Kawthar: two contained river reaches, obvious ferry relationship, meaningful far-bank rest.
  • W06 Ad-Duha: readable night-to-morning density gradient and three distinct thematic movements.
  • W08 Al-Fatiha: unambiguous straight-path grammar, gentle surfaces, broad final landing.
  • W10 An-Nasr: terraces and props consistently lean toward a strong destination gate.
  • W11 Al-Masad: grounded palm clusters and a calm, contained grove sequence.
  • W12 Quraysh: ford, ridge, ferry, summer orchard, and House form a coherent two-season journey.
  • W14 Al-Humazah: repeated hoards vary in form and remain separated by deliberate breathing spaces.
  • W15 Al-'Asr: compact rise–terrace–descent–headland composition with a strong final silhouette.
  • W16 At-Takathur: curtain repetition is a deliberate reveal convention; open finale clearly breaks the pattern.
  • W19 Ayat al-Kursi: boxes of light, guarded sleepers, and aurora maintain a consistent protected-night hierarchy.
